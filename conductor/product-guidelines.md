# Product Guidelines & UX Principles

## Visual Aesthetics & Tone
- **Exam-Native Paper Look**: Warm off-white paper backdrop (`#fbf9f5`), Newsreader/Georgia serif typography for question text and math, crisp borders (`#e2dacd`).
- **Zero LaTeX Exposure**: Teachers must NEVER see LaTeX syntax, backslashes, or code editors anywhere in the UI.
- **Sparingly Used Accent Colors**: Warm amber accent (`#c97a2b`) for review alerts, emerald (`#2e7d32`) for correct key badges.

## Interaction Principles
- **No Scrolling Hell**: Editing controls appear inline or as floating popovers anchored to the target formula.
- **Calm & Trustworthy**: Avoid generic AI SaaS clichés; feel like an actual printed exam paper generator.
